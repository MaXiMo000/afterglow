Nine years, one city.

03 · Hot streets
Some files change every week.
The red beacons mark files edited constantly by very few people. That is where regressions tend to start.

    
04 · Quiet quarters
Nothing here has moved in six years.
Dark windows, dense fog. Nobody remembers why it works, so nobody dares to touch it.

    
05 · The people
Three people wrote most of the core.
Each lantern is a contributor moving between files. If the few that circle the centre left tomorrow, who could explain it?
